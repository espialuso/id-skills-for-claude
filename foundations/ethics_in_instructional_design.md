# Instructional Design Skill: Ethics in Instructional Design

## Overview
Diligence means taking responsibility for instructional design work created with AI assistance. It includes using AI responsibly during the design process, being transparent about how AI contributed, and verifying the quality, accuracy, safety, and appropriateness of the final product before it is deployed.

This skill helps instructional designers apply diligence across three areas: creation, transparency, and deployment. The goal is not simply to use AI effectively, but to remain accountable for the work produced with it.
